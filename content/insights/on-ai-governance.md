The most dangerous AI project is the one that skips the governance conversation.

I've seen this pattern too many times: leadership approves AI tooling because a competitor announced something. The team ships fast. Six months later, a PII leak or a hallucinated output triggers a compliance review.

Speed without governance is just technical debt with legal exposure.

Before the next AI initiative gets approved, ask:
- What data can it touch?
- Who validates the outputs before irreversible action?
- How do you audit what the model decided and why?

Governance isn't the thing that slows AI down. It's the thing that lets you keep using it.
