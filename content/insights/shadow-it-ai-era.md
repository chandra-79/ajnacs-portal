Shadow IT is a demand signal, not a compliance failure. When employees route around official tools, they are telling you that the official tools aren't meeting a real need.\n\nThe AI version of shadow IT is more consequential than previous iterations because the data exposure risk is higher. Pasting customer data, financial projections, or internal strategy documents into a public LLM is an action that can have regulatory and competitive implications that the employee did not intend and may not understand.\n\nThe productive response: treat the shadow AI adoption as evidence of a capability gap in your sanctioned tooling, move faster to close it, and simultaneously educate employees on what data classification means in the context of external AI tools. The combination of supply (a sanctioned tool) and education tends to reduce the risk significantly. Prohibition alone doesn't.
