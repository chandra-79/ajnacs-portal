Technical debt isn't accumulated by accident. It's chosen, usually under pressure.

The problem isn't the choice — sometimes shipping fast is right. The problem is when the debt isn't acknowledged as a debt.

Debt unacknowledged:
- Doesn't get scheduled for repayment
- Compounds silently into the parts of the codebase nobody wants to touch
- Becomes the reason the next feature takes three weeks instead of three days

The teams that manage technical debt well don't avoid it — they name it, estimate it, and track it alongside features.

"We're taking on debt here to ship by Thursday" is a legitimate engineering decision.

"We'll fix it later" with no ticket, no owner, and no date is just hope.
