Platform engineering only works if it's easier than the alternative.

I've seen internal developer platforms built with enormous investment that teams quietly worked around.

The reason is almost always the same: the platform solved the problems the platform team had, not the problems product engineers had day to day.

Before you build:
- Spend a week embedded with the teams who will use it
- Ask what slows them down most, not what you think slows them down
- Build the thing that removes friction first

Platform adoption isn't an engineering problem. It's a product problem.

The best internal platform I've seen was built by someone who spent six months as a product engineer first.
