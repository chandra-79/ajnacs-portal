The trap of managing the way you were managed.

Most first-time engineering managers manage the way their best manager managed them. Or worse — the way their most memorable manager managed them, regardless of whether that was actually good.

The habits that made you an effective senior engineer don't automatically translate:
- Solving problems yourself instead of coaching someone through them
- Being the technical authority rather than building the team's technical judgement
- Measuring your own output rather than your team's output

The transition is harder than most people expect, and almost nobody explains it explicitly before you're already in the role.

The clearest signal you've made the transition: you're proud of something your team built that you had nothing to do with.
