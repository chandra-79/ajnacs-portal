Architecture reviews work when they happen early enough to influence decisions. Late-stage reviews serve primarily as accountability documentation — a record that review happened — rather than as decision support.\n\nThe timing shift that works: a lightweight early review (30 minutes, key stakeholders, main trade-offs only) when the design is 30-40% formed, before significant implementation commitment. A more formal review when the design is complete, focused on catching things the design process missed rather than relitigating the main choices.\n\nThe early conversation is the one that changes outcomes. The late one changes the document.
