In 6 months in 2025, four companies announced quantum computing breakthroughs — from four completely different physical approaches:

→ Microsoft: topological qubits (Majorana-1)  
→ Amazon: cat qubits (Ocelot)  
→ PsiQuantum: photonic qubits (Omega)  
→ Google: superconducting + error correction (Willow)

That's not noise. That's a field that genuinely doesn't know yet which physical substrate wins.

For enterprise planning the practical takeaway is: don't pick a quantum vendor to bet on yet. But **do** start on post-quantum cryptography — NIST finalised the standards in 2024 and your migration timeline is longer than you think.
